# United States: what a government-backed or mandate fund probes

Questions only. Nothing here is tax or legal advice; every rule must be confirmed with a
qualified advisor for the company's facts. Rules were checked on the cited pages on
2026-10-17 and can change.

## Rules to know (each one: verify)

### R&D tax credit and the payroll tax election
- A qualified small business can elect to apply part of its research credit against payroll taxes; for this purpose it must have gross receipts under $5 million for the taxable year and no gross receipts for any year before the 5-taxable-year period ending with that year. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/41 (verify with an advisor)
- The election amount is up to $500,000 for tax years beginning after December 31, 2022 (previously $250,000); it applies first against the employer share of Social Security tax and then the employer share of Medicare tax, with unused amounts carried forward to the next quarter. Source: https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/research-credit-against-payroll-tax-for-small-businesses (verify with an advisor)
- The election is made on Form 6765 by the due date of the originally filed income tax return (including extensions), and the amount is then claimed on Form 8974 with the payroll return. Source: https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/research-credit-against-payroll-tax-for-small-businesses (verify with an advisor)
- The election cannot be made by a company that has already made it for 5 or more preceding taxable years. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/41 (verify with an advisor)

### SBIR / STTR
- SBIR and STTR ("America's Seed Fund") provide non-dilutive funding to US small businesses through 11 participating federal agencies. Source: https://www.sbir.gov/about (verify with an advisor)
- At award, the company must be for-profit with a place of business in the US, more than 50% directly owned and controlled by US citizens or permanent residents (or by other qualifying small businesses), and have no more than 500 employees including affiliates; the work must be performed in the US. Source: https://www.sbir.gov/faq/eligibility-requirements (verify with an advisor)
- Some agencies may make SBIR awards to firms majority-owned by multiple venture capital operating companies, hedge funds or private equity firms; others do not. Source: https://www.sbir.gov/faq/eligibility-requirements (verify with an advisor)
- SBIR and STTR were reauthorized through September 30, 2031 by Public Law 119-83, signed April 13, 2026, ending a six-month lapse in authority. Source: https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12705.html (verify with an advisor)

### QSBS (qualified small business stock, section 1202)
- The gain exclusion applies to stock in a C corporation acquired at original issue (for money, property other than stock, or services) from a corporation that is a qualified small business when the stock is issued. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1202 (verify with an advisor)
- For stock acquired after July 4, 2025, the exclusion is 50% after at least 3 years, 75% after 4 years and 100% after 5 or more years, and the corporation's aggregate gross assets must not exceed $75 million before and immediately after the issuance. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1202 (verify with an advisor)
- At least 80% of assets (by value) must be used in a qualified active business; fields such as health, law, accounting, consulting, financial services, banking, farming, extraction and hotels or restaurants are excluded. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1202 (verify with an advisor)

## What Claire probes

- How much of runway depends on the R&D credit payroll offset or SBIR/STTR awards, and
  what happens if an award is delayed or a solicitation is paused?
- Does the company still meet the qualified-small-business tests for the payroll election
  (gross receipts, years with receipts, years already elected)?
- After this round, will the company still meet SBIR ownership and control rules? If funds
  become the majority owners, does the relevant agency allow that?
- Is the company a C corporation, and has it tracked what investors need for QSBS
  (original issue, gross assets at each issuance, active business)? Has an advisor
  confirmed it?
- Who prepares the credit calculation and the award reporting, and who reviews them?
- Does the R&D credit project description match the product story?

## Red flags

- Payroll offsets or award money counted as recurring revenue.
- A round that changes majority ownership with no check on SBIR eligibility.
- QSBS promised to investors without advisor confirmation.

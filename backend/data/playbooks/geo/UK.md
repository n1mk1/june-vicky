# United Kingdom: what a government-backed or mandate fund probes

Questions only. Nothing here is tax or legal advice; every rule must be confirmed with a
qualified advisor for the company's facts. Rules were checked on GOV.UK on 2026-10-17 and
can change.

## Rules to know (each one: verify)

### R&D tax relief (merged scheme and ERIS)
- For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, companies claim under the merged R&D scheme or Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (ERIS); the older SME scheme covers periods beginning before that date. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/corporation-tax-research-and-development-tax-relief-for-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises (verify with an advisor)
- The merged scheme can be claimed by trading companies chargeable to Corporation Tax with a project meeting the R&D definition; the expenditure credit is taxable as trading income, and some overseas expenditure is restricted. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/research-and-development-rd-tax-relief-the-merged-scheme-and-enhanced-rd-intensive-support (verify with an advisor)
- ERIS is for loss-making, R&D-intensive SMEs: relevant R&D expenditure must be at least 30% of total expenditure (including connected companies); it gives an extra 86% deduction and a payable credit worth up to 14.5% of the surrenderable loss. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/research-and-development-rd-tax-relief-the-merged-scheme-and-enhanced-rd-intensive-support (verify with an advisor)

### SEIS (Seed Enterprise Investment Scheme)
- The company must be established in the UK, its trade must not have been carried on for more than 3 years, it (with subsidiaries) must have gross assets of no more than £350,000 and fewer than 25 full-time-equivalent employees when shares are issued, and it must not have been controlled by another company since incorporation. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seed-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)
- A company can raise a maximum of £250,000 through SEIS, including other de minimis state aid received in the previous 3 years. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seed-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)
- SEIS shares must be paid up in full in cash and be full-risk ordinary shares that are not redeemable and carry no special rights to the company's assets; the money must be spent within 3 years and the rules followed for at least 3 years or investors lose relief. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seed-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)

### EIS (Enterprise Investment Scheme)
- The company must have a permanent establishment in the UK, not be listed or planning to list, not be controlled by another company (no company owning more than 50% of its shares), have fewer than 250 full-time-equivalent employees, and have gross assets of no more than £30 million before and £35 million immediately after the share issue. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/venture-capital-schemes-apply-for-the-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)
- Most companies must receive EIS investment within 7 years of their first commercial sale and can raise up to £10 million in any 12-month period and £24 million over their lifetime (knowledge-intensive companies have different limits). Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/venture-capital-schemes-apply-for-the-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)
- EIS shares must be paid up in full in cash, full-risk ordinary shares that are not redeemable and carry no special rights to assets; the money must be spent within 2 years and the rules followed for at least 3 years. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/venture-capital-schemes-apply-for-the-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)
- HMRC advance assurance on whether a share issue is likely to qualify is optional. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/venture-capital-schemes-apply-for-the-enterprise-investment-scheme (verify with an advisor)

## What Claire probes

- How much of runway depends on R&D relief, and what happens if a claim is reduced or
  paid late? Does the company still meet the ERIS intensity condition after the hiring plan?
- Which investors are relying on SEIS or EIS relief, and has advance assurance been
  obtained?
- Will the share class in this round meet the SEIS/EIS share conditions? A preferred
  class with a liquidation preference or redemption rights may conflict with "no special
  rights to assets"; has an advisor reviewed the structure?
- Could this round leave another company controlling the company, or holding more than
  50% of its shares?
- Is the company within the SEIS/EIS limits on age, gross assets, employees and amounts
  raised, counting earlier rounds?
- Where is R&D performed? Are overseas contractors part of the claim?
- Who prepares the R&D claim and the scheme compliance statements, and who reviews them?

## Red flags

- R&D relief counted as recurring income in burn or runway.
- SEIS/EIS promised to investors on a share class nobody has checked.
- No plan to keep compliant for the 3-year period after investment.

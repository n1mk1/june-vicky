# Generic: what a government-backed or mandate fund probes

Used when the company's country has no dedicated playbook. Claire asks about any public
funding the company relies on, without assuming local rules. Questions only; nothing here
is tax, legal or accounting advice.

## Rules to know (each one: verify)

- Under IFRS (IAS 20), a government grant is recognised only when there is reasonable assurance that the entity will comply with the conditions attached and that the grant will be received. Source: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-20-accounting-for-government-grants-and-disclosure-of-government-assistance/ (verify with an advisor)
- Under IAS 20, grants are recognised in profit or loss on a systematic basis over the periods in which the related costs are expensed, and a grant that becomes repayable is accounted for as a change in accounting estimate. Source: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-20-accounting-for-government-grants-and-disclosure-of-government-assistance/ (verify with an advisor)
- Public programs can pause: for example, US SBIR/STTR authority lapsed for six months before reauthorization on April 13, 2026. Source: https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12705.html (verify with an advisor)

## Grant-dependence questions

- Which grants, credits or public loans does the company receive today, and what share of
  the last 12 months' cash inflows came from them?
- What are burn and runway without any public funding? (Use `burn` excluding non-dilutive
  income and `runway_months`.)
- What conditions are attached to each grant: milestones, matching funds, local hiring,
  location of work or IP, reporting? Which are at risk?
- Can any grant be clawed back or become repayable, and under what circumstances?
- When is each payment expected, and what happens if it is late or reduced?
- Does eligibility depend on ownership, control, size or residence, and could this round
  change any of those? Has an advisor confirmed it?
- How is public funding recorded in the accounts, and who reviews it?
- Is the company dependent on a single program? What happens if it pauses or ends?
- Does the description of the work in grant or credit claims match the product story?

## Red flags

- Grants or credits counted as revenue or as recurring income.
- Runway that only works if the next public payment arrives on time.
- Eligibility conditions nobody has checked against the post-round cap table.

# Canada: what a government-backed or mandate fund probes

Questions only. Nothing here is tax or legal advice; every rule must be confirmed with a
qualified advisor for the company's facts. Rules were checked on the official pages cited
on 2026-10-17 and can change.

## Rules to know (each one: verify)

### SR&ED (Scientific Research and Experimental Development)
- SR&ED is the federal R&D tax incentive program run by the Canada Revenue Agency; it offers an investment tax credit (ITC) and provincial and territorial R&D credits exist alongside it. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program.html (verify with an advisor)
- The basic ITC rate is 15% of qualified SR&ED expenditures. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/sred-claim/investment-tax-credit.html (verify with an advisor)
- Most Canadian-controlled private corporations (CCPCs) can earn a refundable ITC at the enhanced 35% rate on qualified expenditures up to their expenditure limit; the 35% credit on current expenditures is 100% refundable and the credit on capital expenditures is 40% refundable. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/sred-claim/investment-tax-credit.html (verify with an advisor)
- For CCPCs, the expenditure limit is $6 million for tax years beginning after December 15, 2024 ($3 million before), is reduced as prior-year taxable capital rises above $15 million and is nil at $75 million, and must be shared among associated corporations. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/sred-claim/investment-tax-credit.html (verify with an advisor)

### CCPC status (why a round can change refundability)
- A CCPC must, at the end of the tax year, be a private corporation resident in Canada that is not controlled directly or indirectly by one or more non-resident persons, by public corporations, or by any combination of them. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/type-corporation.html (verify with an advisor)
- There is also a combined-ownership test: if all shares owned by non-residents and public corporations were owned by one person, that person must not own enough shares to control the corporation. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/type-corporation.html (verify with an advisor)
- Because the enhanced refundable 35% credit is tied to CCPC status, a financing that gives non-resident investors control, alone or combined, can change the company's SR&ED refundability. Sources: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/type-corporation.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/sred-claim/investment-tax-credit.html (verify with an advisor)

### NRC IRAP (Industrial Research Assistance Program)
- To be eligible for IRAP financial support a business must be incorporated, for-profit and operating in Canada, employ up to 500 people (full-time equivalent), develop and commercialise innovative technology-driven products or services, and be ready to create economic benefits in Canada; meeting the minimums does not guarantee funding. Source: https://nrc.canada.ca/en/support-technology-innovation/financial-support-technology-innovation (verify with an advisor)
- IRAP support usually starts with advice and referrals from an advisor before any funding; work done outside Canada and day-to-day operating costs are not supported. Source: https://nrc.canada.ca/en/support-technology-innovation/financial-support-technology-innovation (verify with an advisor)

### BDC Capital
- BDC Capital describes itself as Canada's largest and most active venture capital investor, and its Seed Venture Fund says it leads seed rounds for Canadian entrepreneurs. Source: https://www.bdc.ca/en/bdc-capital/venture-capital/funds/seed-fund (verify with an advisor)

## What Claire probes

- How much of runway depends on SR&ED refunds and IRAP funding, and what happens if a
  claim is reduced on review or a refund arrives late? (Show burn and runway without them.)
- Is the company a CCPC today, and could this round, including SAFEs converting and any
  non-Canadian investors, change control or the combined-ownership test? Has an advisor
  confirmed it in writing?
- Who prepares the SR&ED claim, what records support it, who signs it off, and have past
  claims been adjusted?
- Does the SR&ED project description match the product story in the deck?
- Is IRAP funding tied to milestones or reporting, and are any conditions at risk?
- Where are staff and IP held, and will the round change that (a new parent company
  abroad, IP moved to another entity)?
- Who leads the round, and is there capacity for follow-on investment?

## Red flags

- Refunds counted as recurring income in burn or runway.
- A planned foreign lead or US parent-company reorganisation with no advice on CCPC status.
- No finance function or reviewer for claims.
